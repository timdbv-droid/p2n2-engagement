A small, private workspace

This independent proof of concept joins relationship snapshots and song interactions using a creator ID that you provide. Display names are never used to merge people.

What the numbers mean

Likes are unique per creator and song. Comments use unique event IDs. Dated events are counted within the selected window, ending on the file’s snapshot date. Undated events appear only under “All imported activity.” The frequency column scales the selected 30- or 90-day count to 30 days; it is not a measure of loyalty.

Follow status comes from each creator’s latest relationship snapshot. Blank means unknown. “Not following back” requires an explicit false value; absence from a partial list is not evidence.

Your 7-day follow-back rule

The default view shows people you follow. A creator becomes ready for review after your chosen waiting period (7 days by default) only when their follow date is known and a relationship snapshot on or after the deadline explicitly says they do not follow you. Older snapshots, missing dates, and unknown statuses go into “Needs a check.” Dates are evaluated at the file’s snapshot date, not a live clock.

Your music preference is separate from engagement. Choose Keep for a personal exception; this does not change their follow status. Likes and comments describe observed engagement with your songs, not audience growth caused by following them. Recheck live status on Suno before taking any action.

Storage and privacy

Your records and notes stay in this tab’s memory. Reloading resets the workspace. Save a JSON backup to retain your work; backups and CSV exports are unencrypted files, so choose a private local folder if you do not want device cloud backup to sync them. Clearing removes this tab’s data, not downloaded files.

The relationship workspace remains local to your browser session. The public song-engagement section fetches a read-only same-site JSON snapshot collected on a schedule by a separate scraper. The scraper does not use your Suno login. Imported relationship notes are never sent to the scraper. Profile links contact Suno only when you choose to open them; sample profile links are disabled.

Song metrics use a separate public-data collector

The song dashboard uses a scheduled Python collector reading publicly accessible Suno metadata. No Apify account, Suno credentials, or paid service is required. Availability and accuracy depend on undocumented public Suno endpoints, which may change or block requests. A missing metric never means zero. This does not retrieve private relationships or comment texts.
